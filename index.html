06 / GOOD TO KNOW

QUESTIONS?
GOOD.

A few things people ask before they take the first step.

Down2Chill is a friends-first app for seeing who is free, what they are down to do, and making a real plan before the moment passes.

No endless scroll, no polished profiles to maintain. Down2Chill is built around a simple question: what are you down to do, and who is down too?

Simply download the app from the App Store or Google Play and follow the on-screen instructions to create your account.

You can add friends by importing your contacts, selecting your preferred connections, and managing your friend list at any time through the app.

Tap into your friends list to see who is online, what they are down to do, and start a plan without the group-text chaos.

Down2Chill brings together three things that make a meetup click: availability, activity preference, and social compatibility — Likeability, Time, and Mood. When those line up, a good plan feels easy.

We prioritize your privacy and security. All data is encrypted and securely stored to protect your information.

Yep. Down2Chill is live on the App Store and Google Play. Download it, add your friends, and see what the group is up to.
